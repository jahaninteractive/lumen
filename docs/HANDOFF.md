What landed:
- **Base map.** `scripts/bootstrap.sh` step 5b fetches six Natural Earth 1:10m GeoJSON layers (coastline, admin-0 boundary lines, rivers and lake centrelines, lakes, roads, populated places simple; about 79 MB) from `nvkelso/natural-earth-vector` at commit `ca96624a56bd078437bca8184e78163e5039ad19`, each checked against a pinned SHA-256, into `vendor/data/map/` (`LUMEN_MAP_DIR`, `Settings.map_dir`). The `land` polygon layer is not fetched (BACKLOG).
- **Index.** `mapdata.MapIndex` at `<library>/cache/map.sqlite`: a manifest per source file tagged `base/`, `regions/`, `overlays/`, and an R-tree. Measured on the six layers: first build 2.72 s for 71,399 features, unchanged `ensure()` 1 ms. Viewport queries decimate by zoom and clip lines to the padded viewport; the Madikwe zoom-9 payload fell from 76,918 to 1,723 bytes. A viewport request that arrives while another builds the index answers `{"building": true}`. A position needs two finite numbers or the feature is skipped; a file that still fails to index is recorded with an error (an OS error as its `strerror`, never a path) and the build carries on.
- **Overlays and regions.** GPX/GeoJSON in `<library>/map/overlays/` (dropped there, or Add in the tab: `POST /api/map/overlays`, `DELETE /api/map/overlays/<name>`). `lumen map-region <name> --bbox S,W,N,E` writes `<library>/map/regions/<name>.geojson` from one Overpass request (4 square degree cap, User-Agent `Lumen/<version> (+repo URL)`, an HTTP-200 `remark` error refused); the server itself never goes online.
- **Geotag.** `POST /api/map/overlays/<name>/geotag` (`{query, offset, max_gap_minutes, dry_run, replace}`) interpolates between the track points bracketing each capture time within `max_gap_minutes` (1-120; `Library.geotag_from_track` defaults it to 10, the HTTP route requires the key); the offset (`+HH:MM`, hours to 14) defaults to exiftool `OffsetTimeOriginal`, else the Mac's zone, and a write (`dry_run: false`) with `offset: null` is refused so the photographer has seen the offset. Writes go through the typed-position path as `location_set` events with `scope: "track"` and `overlay`, never over a camera fix, over typed fixes only with `replace`. The form follows the map's Frames radio and shows the dry-run count and offset before writing.
- **Tab.** Web Mercator canvas, Shown frames / Whole catalog, clusters within 24 px, thumbnails from zoom 11, click opens the frame (narrowing the Review filter to the capture day when the frame is off the sheet), a "Show on map" link in the frame dialog, "n frames have no position" above the geotag form (`unfixed` on `GET /api/map/photos`, with "Add a GPX track to place them" when no overlay is timed), arrows/+/-/0 on the keyboard, a `role="img"` canvas with a side panel as its accessible equivalent.
- **Round 2** (design section "Round 2", plan `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-01-map-tab-round-2.md`). Clicking a cluster fans up to 12 frames onto a ring of 48 px thumbs round its point (flag, then rating desc, then time; radius `max(56, 6 x shown)` px), each a frame hit and listed in Frames in view; `#map-show-all` under the ring sends `ids=<live ids, at most 200>&show_rejected=1` to Review and selects the tab. Pan, zoom, a scope change, Escape or a click on empty map folds it; Enter on the canvas fans the selected or most central cluster. Lone thumbs from zoom 9. Frames radio "Everything ever imported" (`ever`) asks `include_cleared=1`; cleared frames sit in `state.map.photos` with `cleared: true` and `key`, draw as hollow cyan dots, and open `#map-card` (non-modal dialog beside the point, Escape or Close returns focus) instead of the loupe. Backend: `ids` group filter, `cleared` rows and `GET /thumb/cleared/<key>` from the trash snapshots, the scan cached by snapshot paths and mtimes.

Real export: all 117 Sony tags (shutter count 554), no MakerNote warnings.
- The JPEG's XMP `lumen:export` record omits `command` and `source_fingerprint` and keeps only the profile file name; the catalog and sidecars keep the full settings.
- Sony IFD0 lens-correction params (0x7032/0x7035/0x7037) are deliberately not copied; they describe the RAW, and applying them to a corrected render would double-correct.
- There are historical directories named `-p` and `mkdir` at the project root. They were not removed because their ownership/purpose was not established; do not delete them without checking with the user.
